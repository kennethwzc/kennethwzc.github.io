The challenge

Running a gallery and a wine bar means two businesses' worth of admin: artworks, sales, payments and expenses. Accounting alone took 5 hours a week of day-to-day bookkeeping, plus 3 hours at month-end.

What we built

Everything runs through Claude Code

Claude Code sits at the centre, with routines and skills doing the work: reading statements, updating the ledger and CRM, tracking stock and keeping the website live. Scroll to follow a month of admin through the system.

  1. Step 1

    Get the bank statement

    It starts with the business bank statement: every payment in and out of the account.

  2. Step 2

    Extract the information

    Claude Code reads the statement and pulls out each transaction: the date, the amount, and who paid or was paid.

  3. Step 3

    Categorise expenses

    Outgoing payments are sorted into expense categories and recorded in the ledger, with no manual tagging.

  4. Step 4

    Match payments to customers

    Money coming in is matched to the right customer in the CRM, so it's clear who has paid and who still owes.

  5. Step 5

    Post sales and debts

    Sales are posted to the ledger and debts are logged in accounts payable, ready for month-end.

  6. Step 6

    Update stock on every purchase

    When a customer makes a purchase, Claude Code updates the stock in the inventory.

  7. Step 7

    Keep the website live

    A Claude Code routine keeps the website up to date on its own, with no manual edits.

The result

Beyond the hours saved, KYLA can now track the business in detail: which payments are still outstanding, how each artist is performing, and every expense, all in one place.
