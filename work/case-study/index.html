Case study · Bangkok

One connected system for KYLA Gallery & Wine Bar

KYLA runs rotating art exhibitions alongside a wine bar in Silom, Bangkok. We automated the monthly accounting, built a website that keeps itself up to date, and set up an art inventory and a sales CRM, all connected to each other. Accounting alone went from 23 hours a month to 1.

The challenge

Running a gallery and a wine bar means two businesses' worth of admin: artworks, sales, payments and expenses. Accounting alone took 5 hours a week of day-to-day bookkeeping, plus 3 hours at month-end.

What we built

Four pieces, built to work as one system.

Art inventory

Every artwork tracked in one place.

Website

Self-updating, so it stays current without manual edits.

Sales CRM

Every enquiry and sale tracked in one pipeline.

Monthly accounting

Bookkeeping and month-end automated, from 23 hours a month down to 1.

The result

Beyond the hours saved, KYLA can now track the business in detail: which payments are still outstanding, how each artist is performing, and every expense, all in one place.
